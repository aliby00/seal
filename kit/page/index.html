The idea

Every market goes through the scanner. We show what it carries.

A security scanner doesn't judge the traveller. It shows what's in the bag. SEAL does the same with a market's rulebook: the 14 permissions written into its hook address, read and shown, never scored.

Scanner

Left, the market's curtain: one strip per permission, long when the permission is set. Right, the same market x-rayed as luggage. An empty bag is a market with no rulebook.

The parts

Arch, stencil, lockups.

The wordmark is a stencil, like the markings on a cargo crate. Its A is the scanner arch.

Four films, real data

177 Robinhood Chain markets, through the scanner.

Every name and number comes from blocks 86,100,036 to 86,131,118, read on 11 October, about 51 minutes. Each film exists in 16:9 for the timeline and 9:16 for phones.

System

The scanner's false colours.

Baggage scanners paint organic matter orange and metal blue. SEAL uses the same code: orange for what touches trading, blue for what touches liquidity.
